This `please/farewell` repository is a strange alternative to `#sandwiches`. I have so many questions, but it's already 2am and I'm on call tomorrow. That said, some of my best PRs were written in the wee hours of night. So without further ado...

Congratulations on making it this far. I have thoroughly enjoyed my dishwashing internship at the pizza shop since we last broke bread. As delicious as your homemade spätzle was (thanks again for the recipe), my new boss is an actual chef and I couldn't pass up the opportunity to LARP season one of The Bear.

I sincerely hope the tiny island nation of Mauritius affords you the time and space to figure out what you want to do with the rest of your life. May you never have to expend another brain cell on SSDLC, Brex cards, or whatever else the ChangeForce consultants are peddling these days. You were a faithful bullshit umbrella (for many of us), and I'd be remiss not to apologize for all the times I ran out from under your canopy and fucked shit up attempting to shine a light on some entropy (shout out to all our homies in `#凌遲`, assuming they haven't taken that from you yet).

In the end, I suppose I taught you more about PHP extensions than you were able to teach me about emotional intelligence, covariance, and contravariance; but I'll be damned if it wasn't a fun ride. I'm admittedly relieved that I never had to figure out how to generate code for the aggregation builder. Thank you for having the courage to take medical leave and work on yourself, and then encouraging me to do the same. There's some poetry in the fact that you started leading by example long before you became my manager.

And there is solace in seeing you pass the torch on your own terms. Following our unsuccessful dalliances with Eastern European contractors, you assembled a great team in @GromNaN and @paulinevos. @kevinAlbs is undoubtedly a gentleman and a scholar and although his [cracker munching skills](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PfNtzJbP24g) were b-tier at best that wasn't on the engineering lead skill matrix the last time I checked. I'm sure he'll do fine.

It was truly a pleasure to euthanize my career under your watch. Let us both be thankful that AI never had a chance to take our jobs from us. 🤘
